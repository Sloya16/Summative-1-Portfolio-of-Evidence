About Golden Crust

Our story

Golden Crust began in 2013 in a small home kitchen in Benoni. Neighbours kept coming back for the sourdough, and today we run two storefronts.

Mission

To bring honest, handmade baked goods to our community.

Vision

To be the most trusted bakery in town.
